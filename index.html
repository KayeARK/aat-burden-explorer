Interpretation

The dashboard computes modelled infected cattle retained under a reporting rule. Excluded cattle are not assumed disease-free. The main definition is mapped tsetse +50 km, with uncertainty filtering OFF.

A tsetse buffer is a geographical reporting domain, not the complete extent of all animal trypanosomiasis transmission. AAT can persist outside mapped tsetse via movement or mechanical transmission.

The '95% CrI width' refers to the width of a posterior interval for latent prevalence, not a 95% predictive accuracy guarantee. Probability-width and logit-width filters are not interchangeable.

The prevalence surface, tsetse classification and 2020 cattle distribution are treated as fixed input layers. Only the spatial reporting domain and optional uncertainty criterion change.

The prevalence colour scale is display-only. The default is 0–50%; values above a fixed colour limit are shown in the darkest red. Automatic scaling uses the highest retained prevalence in the selected region, rounded up to a whole percent. The map displays the original 25-km Web Mercator cells without altering burden calculations or clicked-cell prevalence values.

Madagascar is excluded from all country and continental results. Cells with missing tsetse classification are displayed as outside finite buffers; their unallocated cattle fractions remain included only in unrestricted totals. A centre explicitly recorded as inside tsetse is treated as inside even if its distance is missing, as in the original R calculation.

Regional membership is assigned by the 25-km prediction-cell centre, using configurable country and latitude rules in the audit script. This is a provisional classification, not a validated epidemiological boundary. Distance-zone cattle allocations remain fractional.

Public research preview: the derived cell-level CSV data are served to the browser and are retrievable even without a download button. Publish only with appropriate permission.

No aggregate posterior credible intervals are calculated; coherent joint posterior prevalence draws would be required for that.
